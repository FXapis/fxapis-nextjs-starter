# Contributing

See the [Contributing section](README.md#contributing) in the README.

## Reporting a problem

Open an [issue](https://github.com/FXapis/fxapis-nextjs-starter/issues) with what you ran,
what you expected, and what happened — the `requestId` from an error if you have one. For an
API, billing or account issue rather than one with this starter, email
[support@fxapis.com](mailto:support@fxapis.com).
